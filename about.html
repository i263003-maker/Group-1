About QuestIndex Search Service

QuestIndex is a pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript game review and search indexing portal. Hover the Search Bubble to expand it, click its green upper notification dot for live Google Form feedback updates, sign in as Mario12 to earn Redeem Points on every search, and level up recursively every 5 points.

Google Form Feedback & Live Notification Dot Config

Link your Google Form URL (for users to submit reviews) and an optional published Google Sheets CSV URL (File → Share → Publish to web → CSV) to fetch live review notifications directly into the Search Bubble dot.
